Privacy policy

What FireMD does with your data

FireMD is a Markdown notes app. Your notes are yours: you can export them at any time, and deleting a note deletes it. This page says exactly what is stored, why, who processes it, how long it stays, and how to get rid of it.

Last updated: 23 November 2026.

Who is responsible

FireMD (firemd.app) is the data controller for the data described here. For any privacy question, or to exercise a right, write to iuvalgoldansky@gmail.com.

What is stored

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and profile picture, plus the account identifier Google gives the app. There is no password: authentication happens on Google's side.
  • Your notes. The Markdown you write, note titles, folders, tags, canvases, and the access list of each note or folder (who is owner, who can edit, who can read, and whether a link grants access).
  • Collaboration data. The sync blocks that carry edits between devices, and short-lived presence records (cursor position, selection, a display name and a colour) that disappear when you stop editing.
  • Usage counters. How many reads, writes, deletes, stored bytes and transferred bytes your account used, per day and per month, because that is what the free quota and the prepaid balance are calculated from.
  • Billing records. Your prepaid balance and an immutable ledger of top-ups. We never see or store your card: the payment is processed by Polar, our merchant of record.
  • Product events and crash reports. A small fixed set of event names (for example "note created", "export") and, when something breaks, the error name and a truncated message, so we can fix it. No free text, no third-party analytics scripts, no advertising identifiers.
  • Local data in your browser. Your session, your theme and layout preferences, the offline cache, and — if you write before signing in — a local draft that never leaves the browser unless you choose to save it.

Why it is processed

  • To provide the service: syncing, storing and sharing your notes.
  • To authenticate you and protect your account.
  • To measure usage, apply the free quota and charge the prepaid balance.
  • To diagnose failures and prevent abuse of the service.
  • To comply with tax and accounting obligations, which our merchant of record handles.

We do not sell personal data, do not build advertising profiles, and do not use your notes to train models.

Who processes it

  • Google Cloud / Firebase — authentication, database, file hosting and monitoring (data stored in the United States, nam5).
  • Polar — merchant of record for card payments: it processes the payment, issues the receipt and handles sales tax and VAT. We only receive the confirmation of the order and its amount.
  • Our domain and DNS provider — to serve the site and its certificates.

No other party receives your data. Agents you connect on purpose (a CLI or an AI agent) act with the permissions you grant them, through the same security rules that apply to a person.

How long it is kept

Notes, folders, tags and access lists Until you delete them, or your account.
Sync blocks between devices Compacted continuously; old blocks are pruned automatically.
Presence (cursor, selection) Seconds to minutes; it is designed to disappear.
Usage counters Kept per day and per month, for quota and billing.
Product events and crash reports Short-lived; they are pruned automatically.
Backups Daily snapshots with a 7-day retention, plus a 7-day point-in-time recovery window.
Local draft in the browser Until you save or discard it; it never leaves your device otherwise.

Your rights

  • Export — the app exports every note as Markdown with its folder structure, whenever you want. The format stays Markdown.
  • Delete — deleting a note removes it and its content. To delete your whole account, write to the address above; it is done within 30 days, including the notes, counters and billing records we are not required to keep for tax purposes.
  • Access and correction — your data is visible in the app; you can correct any of it, and ask us for a copy.
  • Object or restrict — write to us and we will explain or stop the processing in question, unless a legal obligation requires it.
  • Revoke access — removing a person from a note or folder cuts their access immediately, enforced by the database rules.

How it is protected

All traffic is HTTPS with HSTS. Access to each note is decided by database security rules on the server, not by the client: the browser cannot widen its own permissions. The prepaid balance can only be written by our backend. Backups and point-in-time recovery are enabled.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, the date at the top changes and, for anything material, we announce it in the app before it takes effect.
