What FireMD is

A notes editor that treats Markdown as the real format, not as a preview. Four things it does well:

Simultaneous editing

A CRDT over Firestore with compaction and remote cursors. Two people in the same paragraph do not overwrite each other.

Real permissions

Owner, Can edit and Read only, per note and per folder. Revoking access cuts it immediately.

Built for agents

Plain-text output, atomic edits guarded by an etag, and its own identity with the minimum permissions you grant.

Offline and installable

Works as an app. The persistent Firestore cache keeps what you have read and queues writes until the connection returns.

Free every day, prepaid when you need more

Nothing expires as a trial and no card is required to start. The free quota renews every day at 00:00 UTC; the prepaid balance is only used for the usage above it.

How paying works

  • Card payments are handled by Polar, the merchant of record for every purchase. Polar processes the payment, issues the receipt and handles sales tax and VAT at checkout.
  • Prepaid only. You add credit, and usage is deducted from it. There is no recurring subscription; automatic top-up is optional and can be turned off at any time.
  • Unused credit is refundable within 14 days of purchase. After that it stays on the balance until it expires, 24 months after purchase.
  • The balance cannot leave the product. It pays for usage inside FireMD only: it cannot be withdrawn, transferred to another account, gifted or resold.
  • No discount codes are accepted at checkout, so the amount charged is always the pack you chose.

Questions

What happens to what I write before signing in?

It stays in your browser. When you sign in with Google we offer to save it to your account; if you do not, the draft stays in this browser until you discard it.

How is a credit used?

One balance, one price list. Usage above the free daily quota is deducted at the published unit prices; a day under the quota costs nothing. The app shows the balance and the estimated cost as you go.

How do I revoke someone's access?

From the share panel of the note or the folder. The cut is immediate: the security rules stop accepting writes from that person.

How does the CLI and MCP server work?

The CLI returns plain text and edits with an etag so it never overwrites someone else's change. The full guide is in agent.md.

How do I control my spending?

The free quota renews every day. You choose each pack manually; automatic top-up is optional and off by default. Nothing is charged without a purchase you made.

Can I export my vault?

Yes. The app exports your notes as Markdown with their folder structure. The format stays Markdown, never a proprietary one.
